What happens when AI agents create societies?

Exploring the nature of agents, artificial societies, and the social and political behavior that emerges between them.

01 / About

AI agents and
their societies.

Hi, I am an independent researcher. I want to study how different agents come together and create societies. I'm interested in the micro and macro dynamics that emerge, and what artificial societies have in common with human ones. Focused on using anthropology, political science, sociology, Agent-Based Modeling and more to study these artificial dynamic societies.
