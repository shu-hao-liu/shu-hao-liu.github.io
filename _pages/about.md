I am a student researcher interested in AI. Specifically, I am interested in investigating the reciprocal relationships that occur when pairing AI with different fields. I am fascinated by what AI can bring to other fields, and what these fields can bring to AI. Some areas I am interested include: generative agent-based modeling, political science, anthropology, and bio-inspired AI.
